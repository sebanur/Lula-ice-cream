## Brand & Style
The design system reflects the sensory delight of traditional artisanal gelato-making: creamy textures, slow churns, vibrant natural ingredients, and joyful summer moments. The identity balances gourmet elegance with warm, approachable indulgence. 

The aesthetic is Tactile-Playful Modern. It combines rich, organic culinary hues with soft, pillowy depth, generous rounded architecture, and deliberate micro-interactions that mirror the satisfying scoop of fresh gelato. Interface surfaces mimic matte ceramic bowls, warm waffle textures, and velvety creams rather than cold synthetic glass. Every visual touchpoint reinforces indulgence, small-batch quality, and neighborhood hospitality.

## Colors
The palette evokes natural, wholesome gelato flavors:
- **Primary (`#7FA976` - Bronte Pistachio):** Anchors main actions, fresh callouts, and primary badges. Conveys fresh organic ingredients and craft mastery.
- **Secondary (`#F47C7C` - Wild Strawberry Cream):** Drives conversion triggers, cart updates, celebratory alerts, and promotional micro-badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E8A858` - Roasted Honey Waffle):** Highlights ratings, baked accompaniments, artisanal badges, and active toggle accents.
- **Neutral (`#3D2619` - Cacao Ganache):** High-contrast typography and icon base. Replaces stark carbon blacks with a velvety, espresso-chocolate depth.

### Contextual Canvas Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF7F2` (Rich Clotted Cream).
- **Surface Level 1:** `#FFFDF9` (Vanilla Chiffon), used for cards and sheets.
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F3EDE2` (Toasted Milk), used for input wells and inactive selectors.
- **Accent Berry (`#8D4B74`):** Seasonal markers and dietary certifications (Vegan, Dairy-Free).
- **Accent Mint (`#A8D5BA`):** Cold-chain logistics, insulated packaging status, and allergen guarantees.

## Typography
Typography creates a dialogue between editorial indulgence and effortless readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue):** Chosen for its sculptural, rhythmic quirks and hearty warmth. Bold weights highlight product titles, tasting notes, and artisanal headers with an inviting, hand-crafted posture.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and high legibility at dense mobile viewport scales.
- **Numerical Details:** Price points utilize `Epilogue` semibold to preserve physical packaging character, while volume metrics (`500ml`, `1000g`) remain in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for quick scanning during rapid checkout.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Max container width is 1280px with centered auto-margins, 24px gutters, and 32px canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Fluid multi-column flow with 20px gutters and 24px margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Critical quick-toggles (Serving Type, Scoop Size) transition into horizontal thumb-friendly carousels with edge fade masks.

Vertical rhythm stays open and airy (`space-xl` between showcase clusters) to create breathing room around rich food imagery, while action clusters maintain snug proximities (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) for frictionless cart assembly.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered through soft, pillowy ambient shadows tinted with cacao and toasted cream pigments instead of cold grey alpha channels:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF7F2` canvas with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Ambient Plates):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.03);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Controls & Active Chips):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -4px rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Slide-out Cart & Modal Sheets):** `box-shadow: -8px 0 36px 0 rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.12);` complemented by a warm backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(61, 38, 25, 0.25);`

Tactile press states compress elevation by reducing blur, shifting the element 1.5px downwards, and applying a soft inner glow to mimic physical feedback.

## Shapes
Forms are generous, organic, and welcoming. Pill-shaped geometry dominates controls and pills, drawing inspiration from smooth scoops and rounded wooden spoons.

- **Primary Cards & Panels:** Apply `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for cozy, cushioned containment.
- **Buttons, Serving Chips & Badges:** Use `rounded-full` (9999px) to invite direct touch.
- **Image Frames:** Utilize smooth continuous squircles or asymmetric organic radii (`1.75rem 1.75rem 1rem 1.75rem`) to echo hand-sculpted gelato dollops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Pint / Sepete Ekle"):** Background `#7FA976`, text `#FFFDF9`, full pill radius, `1rem 1.75rem` padding. Deepens slightly on hover with a buoyant scale (`transform: scale(1.02)`). Includes an optional trailing bubble counter for quantity.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1.5px solid #E8A858`, text `#3D2619`.
- **Tertiary Quick-Add ("+"):** 44x44px circular pill button in `#F3EDE2` with `#3D2619` glyph, turning to `#F47C7C` with white glyph upon selection.

### Serving Type Selectors (Külah vs. Bardak)
Dual segment controller with a pill toggle track in `#F3EDE2`. Active selection slides an illuminated `#FFFDF9` pill with micro-illustration (Crispy Cone / Eco Cup), elevated with Level 1 pillowy shadow and text colored `#3D2619`. Inactive option rests at 60% opacity.

### Flavor & Dietary Badge Tags
Miniature rounded-full labels (`label-sm`, letter-spacing `0.04em`, uppercase).
- **Pistachio Batch / House Signature:** Background `#7FA976` at 15% tint, text `#496843`.
- **Vegan Sorbet:** Background `#A8D5BA` at 25% tint, text `#2C5E43`.
- **Seasonal / Limited:** Background `#F47C7C` at 15% tint, text `#9E2D2D`.

### Product Cards
Built on `#FFFDF9` with a subtle 1px border (`#EFE8DC`) and Level 1 elevation. Structure from top to bottom:
1. Pinned dietary badges and cold-shipping icon.
2. Centered imagery with soft circular ambient flavor glow behind the product scoop.
3. Title (`headline-sm`) in `#3D2619` paired with single-origin ingredient origin.
4. Flavor note description (`body-sm`) in `#3D2619` at 75% opacity.
5. Bottom footer split between price (`Epilogue` 20px) and a pill-shaped quick-add action.

### Slide-Out Cart Drawer
Fixed 440px wide right-hand drawer (fullscreen on mobile) styled in `#FAF7F2`. Features a top thermal packaging tracker ("Shipped in Dry Ice & 100% Recyclable Thermal Box") in `#A8D5BA` tinted card, an interactive list of items with scoop-level customization tags, and a sticky checkout footer displaying delivery window selectors and total price.

### Form Inputs & Number Steppers
Inputs feature full-rounded pill contours with `#F3EDE2` fills, transitions to `#7FA976` border outlines with 3px `#7FA976`/15% halo on focus. Number steppers (`-` / `+`) are encapsulated into a single unified pill unit with high-contrast tactile tap zones.
